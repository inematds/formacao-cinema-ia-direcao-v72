02 · How to build the storyboard

A header with the fixed decisions and one line per shot.

1. Header

Scene, anchor and emotion come from the card. The character sheet and the look go in one line each, summarized: this is the snippet that repeats in every prompt.

If a line in the header changes midway, every shot changes with it.

2. The shots

The shot list from lesson 7 grows to 30 seconds: 6 to 10 shots, each with size, angle, one movement, duration, action and sound.

Vary it: at least 3 sizes, 2 angles and 3 movements. Open by setting the scene, move in closer at the turn, and close on an image that works as a still.

3. Check before you generate

The page checks the whole package in seconds. Fixing a line of text costs nothing; redoing a generation costs time and credits.

Only take to the generator the storyboard that has already answered OK.

4. The bridge to course 1

In course 1, each line of this storyboard becomes a prompt, with the character sheet as a reference and the look as a fixed block.

Keep the project folder: it is the one you open in the first lesson there.
