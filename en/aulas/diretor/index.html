Lesson 1 · 15 min

The director and the machine: what AI does and what stays yours

The 2026 video generator creates image, motion and sound. It doesn't know what the audience should feel or why the camera is there. This lesson separates what the machine carries out from what you decide, and ends with the director's card for your scene.

Start the lesson

  2. 2Choose the scene's anchor: character, product or rhythm
  3. 3Answer the four director questions before generating
  4. 4Keep the project outside any platform

Goal: separate what you decide from what the AI carries out, and fill in the director's card for a scene before generating anything.

02 · What is yours, in four ideas

The example is the course's scene: Dona Lia opens the neighborhood bakery at 4:50 a.m.

1. AI carries out direction

The tool generates pixels; you are the one who generates meaning. A scene with no internal logic comes out pretty and empty, and a new model won't fix that.

Intention has a practical definition: being able to justify every choice. If the answer to "why this angle?" is "it just came out that way," the scene gets weaker.

2. The scene's anchor

Before any image, name the anchor in one sentence. It changes with the format and decides angle, light and composition.

In a film, the anchor is the character. In an ad, it's the product, the idea or the transformation. In a music video, it's the rhythm.

Anchor of the batchDona Lia. Everything in the scene serves the quiet pride of someone who wakes up before the city.

3. The four questions

Why this angle? Why this movement? Why this duration? Why this light? Angle gives power or fragility, movement gives emotion, duration gives weight and light gives mood.

A choice you can't justify weakens the scene. Lessons 2, 3 and 4 go deeper into each question.

4. The tool changes, the direction stays

The Sora app was shut down on 04/26/2026 and the API on 09/24/2026. Video tools disappear, change names or change prices.

That's why your project lives in a folder of your own: director's card, character sheet, look and shot list in text. With them, any generator of the moment can carry out the same scene.

03 · Templates to copy

The director's card and a request for the AI assistant to test your choices.

cartao.txt (template)
Scene: <the scene in one sentence>
Format: <film, ad or clip, and where it will be shown>
Anchor: <what holds the eye: character, product or rhythm>
Emotion: <what the audience should feel>
Angle: <which one and why>
Movement: <which one and why>
Duration: <how long each shot lasts and why>
Light: <which one and why>
Request to the AI assistant (the director's questions)
Read my director's card below. For each field Angle, Movement, Duration and Light, ask me a question that forces me to justify the choice based on what the audience should feel. Don't suggest answers and don't rewrite the card; just ask.
